hunchnet

A brain-inspired model that learns melodies and text patterns online, and shows you its predictions and its surprise as it goes. Everything runs on your device.

Status: the Rust core now runs in your browser as WebAssembly. Press a note below and watch the prediction and surprise; richer visualisation is coming. Source on GitHub.
