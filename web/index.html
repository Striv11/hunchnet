hunchnet

A brain-inspired model that learns melodies and text patterns online, and shows you its predictions and its surprise as it goes. Everything runs on your device.

Status: the Rust core runs in your browser as WebAssembly. Play a note or a preset melody and watch the prediction and surprise, then let the model dream. Source on GitHub.
